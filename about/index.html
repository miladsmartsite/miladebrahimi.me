About

I help people engineer their time and lead themselves.

I'm Milad Ebrahimi: productivity coach, author and international trainer, and the founder of ME Academy.

For more than ten years I've coached people on the same few questions: where does my time go, what do I actually want, and why does my life feel out of balance? The answers became three books, Time Engineering, The Balance Book and Self-Coaching Blueprint.

I'm a civil engineer by background, and it shows in how I work: I treat a week like a structure that can be designed, measured and improved, not something you simply survive. Years in marketing and operations roles taught me the same lesson inside companies: most problems are system problems.

Today I share one practical idea from my books every day on YouTube, in under 30 seconds, and I build ME.Coach, the app that turns the book's tools into a daily habit. I live and work in Muscat, Oman.
