About Me & Resume

Milad Ebrahimi

Operations Manager with over 9 years of experience in business operations, leadership, marketing, and organizational development, focused on building systems, improving performance, and driving sustainable growth.

01

Profile

The path here didn't start with operations. It started with running an independent education practice for six years — the kind of work where you're responsible for everything at once, because there's no one else to hand it to.

From there, the work moved into marketing: first as a specialist executing campaigns, then managing digital marketing, then leading marketing communications end to end. Three roles, three companies, each one a step up in scope — content, lead generation, brand visuals, campaign strategy.

The shift into operations happened at Perla Engineering — starting in marketing, then moving into an operations role once it became clear that the more valuable work was further upstream: not just producing content or campaigns, but building the systems that let an organization actually run — daily reporting, material requests, a defined operating rhythm for the role itself.

That practice is now continuing at Tathmeer Properties, consolidating reporting across a larger project portfolio. Alongside it, three books have been written over the past decade — on time, on balance, and on self-coaching — a separate but related thread about how people (not just organizations) actually get better at their work.

The practice

Where operations, systems, and leadership meet.

The throughline across all of it: understand how something actually works, find where it's breaking, and build the smallest system that fixes it — whether that means an operational process, a piece of marketing, or a place AI genuinely changes the outcome. Not a title, not a pitch — just the way the work has consistently gone.
