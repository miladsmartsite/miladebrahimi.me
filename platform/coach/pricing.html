Billing questions

How does the free trial work?

You get every feature for 7 days. No card is needed to start. After 7 days you choose a plan to keep editing. Everything you created stays safe and readable.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

What is the Coaching plan?

Everything in Pro plus one online 1:1 session a month with Milad Ebrahimi, author of Self-Coaching Blueprint, to review your Wheel, your cycle and your plan. Places are limited.

Can I pay in Omani rial?

Yes. Prices are shown in Omani rial and US dollars. Card payment and local options are available; if you prefer bank transfer, choose a plan in the app and follow the instructions.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. If ME.Coach is not for you, write to us within 14 days of your first payment and we refund it in full. See terms and refunds.

Is there a discount for teams or schools?

Yes. Write to info@miladebrahimi.me for group pricing.
