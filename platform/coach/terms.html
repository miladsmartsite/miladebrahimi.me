Terms and refunds

Last updated 16 November 2026

The service

ME.Coach is a self-coaching tool. It does not replace medical, psychological, legal or financial advice. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.

Accounts

You are responsible for your account and for keeping your sign-in methods safe. One account per person.

Free trial

New accounts get 7 days of full access. No payment details are needed for the trial. After the trial your data stays readable; editing needs an active plan.

Plans and payment

Prices are shown in Omani rial (OMR) and US dollars (USD) and may include taxes where the payment provider applies them. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You can cancel at any time; your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.

The Coaching plan includes one online 1:1 session per month, booked in advance. Unused sessions do not carry over.

Refunds

If you are not happy, email info@miladebrahimi.me within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. Renewals can be refunded within 7 days if the service was not used after the renewal.
