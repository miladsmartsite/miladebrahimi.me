Privacy policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

Who we are

ME.Coach is operated by Milad Ebrahimi, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. Contact: info@miladebrahimi.me.

What we collect

Your account details (name, email; a password hash if you use a password; a passkey public key if you use Face ID or Touch ID; your Google account email and ID if you sign in with Google).

The content you create in the app: goals, tasks, habits, check-ins, wheel scores and similar records.

Payment status (plan and end date). Card details are handled by the payment provider and never reach our server.

Basic technical logs needed to run and protect the service.

How we use it

Only to provide ME.Coach to you: store and sync your data, send the reminders you turn on, and manage your subscription. We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.

Where it is stored

On our hosting server in a private folder for your account. Data you keep on your device stays in your browser storage until you sign out.

Your choices

You can download all your data at any time from Settings. To delete your account and data, email us from your account email and we delete it within 30 days.

Cookies

We use one essential sign-in cookie and your browser storage to keep you signed in and work offline. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
