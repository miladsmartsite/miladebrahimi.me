context: >
  Tathmeer runs a large portfolio of active construction projects, each tracked separately
  through monthly reports. There was no single view that showed how the whole portfolio was
  actually doing at once.
problem: >
  Getting a straight answer to "how are we doing across everything" meant manually reassembling
  separate reports every time the question came up.
challenge: >
  Building something genuinely useful fast — without introducing a new platform — while running
  structured discovery across the business to understand what each part actually needed to see.
approach: >
  Started with discovery, not design: structured conversations across departments before building
  anything. Then designed one shared data model to sit behind every dashboard and KPI, so every
  number traces back to a single formula and a single owner.
system: >
  A portfolio-level dashboard for management, group-level views for project clusters, a weighted
  subcontractor performance scorecard, and an interactive management-review tool — built as a
  lightweight reporting layer rather than a new piece of software.
howItWorks: >
  Project-level data feeds one shared data model. The data model feeds every dashboard and
  scorecard. Once data is validated, it becomes the basis for weekly and monthly management
  review.
tools: ["Google Sheets", "Google Drive", "Odoo (integration in progress)"]
implementation: >
  Delivered inside the first two weeks of the engagement: a working portfolio dashboard,
  group-level dashboards, a subcontractor scorecard, the underlying data model, and a phased
  roadmap for deeper ERP integration.
