context: >
  Marketing activity generated leads, but there was no consistent system for logging them,
  following up, or handing qualified leads to Sales with enough context to act on.
problem: >
  Leads risked falling through the cracks between being generated and being acted on, and content
  was being posted without a consistent process connecting it to lead capture.
challenge: >
  Owning two different jobs at once — producing content and capturing and qualifying leads —
  without either one slipping when the other got busy.
approach: >
  Split the role into two explicitly owned tracks, each with its own daily and weekly rhythm, and
  built one shared system connecting them: content that generates leads, and a process that makes
  sure every lead gets a timely reply and a clean handoff.
system: >
  A lead-tracking system with defined statuses (new, contacted, meeting, lost), reply templates,
  and a weekly reporting rhythm, paired with a content calendar with clear rules — including a
  strict rule against posting anything that isn't real project work.
howItWorks: >
  A new lead is logged within an hour and replied to within two, using a template. Qualified
  leads are handed to Sales the same day with full context. Anything unanswered after 24 hours
  gets followed up. A weekly summary — total leads, source, status — goes to management.
tools: ["Google Sheets", "WhatsApp", "Social media"]
implementation: >
  In active use as the standing process for content and lead handling.
